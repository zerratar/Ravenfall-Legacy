## What makes it Lumbridge

- **A compact town:** grey stone houses with red tiled roofs, close round the castle, joined by grey stone roads that
  branch everywhere.
- **Wooden rail fences** along the roads and round every field and pen.
- **Trees everywhere:** a dark forest west of the castle (with dead trees), clumps along the roads, trees in fields.
- **The river Lum:** a stone arch bridge with low walls, rocks, reeds, lily pads and ducks; steep grassy banks.
- **The castle:** grey stone, crenellated, blue and white banners, a courtyard with fountains and statues.
- **The church and graveyard:** a white picket fence, crosses and gravestones, a tomb at the south exit, paths.
- **Farms north:** fenced fields (cows, calves, sheep), a windmill with wheat, a chicken coop, Fred's farmhouse.
- **Across the river:** the goblins' houses on the east bank, then the fenced edge of the desert (Al Kharid).
- **The swamp south:** small islands in shallow water, dead trees, reeds and rocks; the mines in it.
