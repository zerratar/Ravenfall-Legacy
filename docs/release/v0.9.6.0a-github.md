# Ravenfall v0.9.6.0a Release

**Quests, Ravebridge with hero mode, your own boats, the banana island and village decor. The biggest update Ravenfall has had: there is finally something to do together beyond training.**

> **Running the bot yourself?** Download the new `RavenBot.7z` (or `RavenBot-linux.7z`) for `!quest`, `!hero`, `!ravebridge`, `!boat`, `!regatta`, `!decor` and the rest below. The bot most streamers use (the one hosted by us) is already updated.

> **Auto join costs coins again.** Joining raids and dungeons automatically was free by mistake since 0.9.5. It is charged once per raid or dungeon again, in one go for everyone joining.

---

## What is new?

### Quests
- **Daily and weekly quests** for every player: `!quest` shows yours and what they pay.
- **Stream quests** the whole stream works on together, started by the streamer from the quest bar, the quest window or any quest board in the world. Everyone who helps gets a share, and the village gets its cut.
- **Quest chains** with a story: the barkeep's runaway cat, the fish that got away, the village founder and the shipwright. Finishing a chain unlocks decor for the village.
- **Achievements and titles.** Titles show on the name tag; pick yours with `!title`, see your progress with `!achievements`.
- **Streamer quests.** Streamers post their own bounties and stream goals in the quest window, with coins and items as rewards.
- **Stories that open islands.** The runestone, the banana island and the road west open new places for the stream, one after the other.

### Ravebridge and hero mode
A whole new island, laid out as a tribute to a certain castle town by a river. It opens through the stories (the runestone, then the road west), and once in a while goblins rise up and open it on their own.
- **The streamer plays by hand.** Walk with WASD or a click, fight with the mouse, roll out of the way with Space, switch the camera with V. Four fighting styles with four skills each, potions, loot, a shopkeeper's cart and gear that only counts on the island.
- **People and quests.** Talk to the islanders with E. There are quests for the duke, the captain, the cook, a ghost, a vampyre in his manor and more, plus repeatable bounties.
- **Hold the line.** Captain Hargrove's waves are the endless challenge: how far can you get? Bosses, telegraphed attacks, and **a daily challenge** that is the same for every streamer that day. Best runs go on the leaderboard on the website.
- **Chat fights with you.** Viewers join with `!ravebridge` (or `!rb`), and `!hero` lets them `heal`, `taunt`, `cheer` and `revive` you. Between waves chat votes you a boon with `!hero 1`, `2` or `3`; with nobody voting, the streamer clicks the card they want.
- **A proper welcome.** The first time a stream opens it, Ravebridge rises from the sea. A short introduction explains the controls; both can be seen again from the settings.
- There is a big map on M, and the minimap and map can be clicked to walk there. The island switches itself off while nobody uses it, so it costs nothing the rest of the time.

### Your own boat
- **Five boats:** the Rowboat from the shipwright's chain, then the Fishing Boat, Sloop, Brigantine and Galleon, built at the shipwright on Away as your Sailing level allows. Boats go in their own equipment slot.
- **Sail anywhere** with your boat equipped: `!sail <island>` goes straight there, `!sail` alone wanders the sea. **Fish at sea** with `!fish` to drop anchor: sea fish, a few fish found only at sea, and the odd message in a bottle. `!boat` shows your boat, `!boat build` builds one at the shipwright.
- **Bring friends aboard.** `!boat invite` and `!boat join` ask, `!boat accept` or `decline` answers. Captains earn Sailing experience and a cut of their guests' catch.
- **Make it yours.** Hulls, sails and flags are earned through play (the regatta, treasure, the pirates' chest) shown with `!boat looks` and changed with `!boat sails 3`, `!boat flag 2` and so on. Clan members can fly the clan's flag and sails in the clan's colours. Try every look on the website's new boat designer.
- The ferry now has a top speed; at high Sailing levels your own boat is the fast way round.

### The banana island
The Mangrove island opens through the banana story, and it is full of nonsense:
- **Monkeys** guarding a banana pile that the whole stream feeds with `!banana`.
- **Pirate treasure** to dig up with `!dig`.
- **The regatta:** `!regatta` races everyone's boats round the island, with bottles, reefs, cannonballs, a Kraken, and viewers meddling with `keg`, `wind`, `swim` and `propeller`.
- **A pirate raid** (`!pirates`) to fight off, in hero mode if you like.
- Banana items: Banana Bread, Banana Split, the Golden Banana, the Bananarang and a Monkey Pet.

### Village decor
- **The village island is yours to shape.** Its trees, bushes and flowers can be moved or removed (removing gives half their worth back), and a new **Decor** tab in build mode places fences, lights, market stalls, farm pieces, banners, statues and more.
- Decor unlocks with the town hall and village level, quest chains, the seasons and the treasury.
- **Viewers can gift decor** with their loyalty points: `!decor gift`.
- The website shows each village's town.

### Islands and the world
- **Ironhill reimagined:** an iron mountain round the runestone's cave, a dwarf hold with a forge-master, scorched dragon country with glowing lava cracks, and a red dragon asleep on the summit.
- Idle enemies wander about instead of standing still.
- Quest marks show who has something for you: over Ravebridge's islanders, the runestone, the banana island's sign and the tavern's quest giver.
- Shaded slopes and the island cliffs are no longer black, and Ironhill, the tavern and the arena lost their blotchy lighting.

### The website
- **A new character customizer** in 3D (with a 2D fallback), including separate stubble and scar colours.
- **A quest page** for players and streamers: today's quests, chains, achievements and your stream's stories.
- **A boats page** with the boat designer, and a **hero mode page** with the all-time and daily leaderboards.

## Improved
- `!gift` and `!send` take a number of sets: "abraxas armor set 5" or "5 sets of abraxas armor".
- Name tags show your title, fade when they would cover a nearer one, and stay put on a moving boat.
- Tooltips grow wider for long (enchanted) item names instead of wrapping them.
- The game keeps the server's copy of every inventory and checks it, so items no longer go missing or double up after a hiccup.
- Crafting checks with the server before saying an ingredient is missing.
- The shortcut bar was redesigned, and the settings cog in the top right corner is back.
- F1 hides the new UI too.

## Fixed
- An enchantment's skill bonus no longer vanishes from the skill panel on a level up.
- Mages, healers and archers no longer freeze on their last cast frame when a raid or dungeon ends.
- Raid bosses no longer spawn on islands that are not open to the stream.
- The game could freeze when its connection to the bot dropped and came back at the wrong moment.
- The game no longer reconnects to the bot every 30 seconds while chat is quiet.
- Missing item icons, and item sets that could not be found the first time.
