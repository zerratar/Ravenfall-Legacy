# 🐦‍⬛ Ravenfall v0.9.5 is out!

This is the biggest update Ravenfall has ever had. Here are the highlights:

🌊 **A whole new look.** An ocean with waves, volumetric clouds, dense grass and flowers, trees swaying in the wind, butterflies, gulls and fireflies, and moonlit nights.
⛈️ **Weather.** Rain, snow and thunderstorms roll in by themselves. Ask chat what's coming with `!weather`.
🎵 **Music and sound.** Music for every situation (islands, night, rain, sailing, dungeons, raid bosses...), and the world finally has sound: axes, fires, waves, birds and thunder.
🏰 **Build your village.** A new build mode: place and move your houses, draw roads, and upgrade your town hall all the way from a longhouse to a castle.
🔌 **A more reliable connection.** Progress is kept until the server confirms it, and the game reconnects by itself without losing anything.
🖥️ **A rebuilt interface** with movable windows, a new settings window, Twitch sign in, and Swedish as the first translation.

**Streamers, please note:**
• Everyone has to sign in **once** after updating.
• The game updates itself when you start it. New install? Grab it from the link below.
• Running the bot yourself? Download the new `RavenBot.7z` too. If you use our hosted bot, you don't need to do anything.
• On a weaker PC? Potato mode turns the new effects off.

📜 Full changelog and downloads: https://github.com/zerratar/Ravenfall-Legacy/releases/tag/v0.9.5.0a-alpha

Thank you all for your patience and support while we worked on this one. Let us know what you think, and report any bugs here on Discord! 💜
