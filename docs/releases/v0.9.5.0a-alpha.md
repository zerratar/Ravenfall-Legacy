# Ravenfall v0.9.5.0a Release

**The biggest update Ravenfall has had: a new engine version, a new and much more reliable connection to the server, a rebuilt interface, a visual overhaul with weather, music and sound effects, and a village you can finally build yourself.**

New Unity version: **6000.7.0b4**, using the new **CoreCLR** scripting backend on Windows and Linux.

> **Please update the local bot too** if you run it yourself (`RavenBot.7z`, or `RavenBot-linux.7z`). The bot most streamers use (the one hosted by us) is already updated.
> Everyone signs in once more after this update, see "Before you update" below.

---

## Before you update

- **Sign in again once.** Sign-in tokens are now signed by the server, so old ones stop working. A password saved by "Remember me" signs you in once more and is then replaced by a device token. Logging out removes the token.
- **The new graphics are on by default.** On a weak PC, turn on potato mode or switch off single effects under Settings > Graphics.
- **Check Settings > Bot messages.** The filters there were saved the wrong way round before.
- **Village:** your houses stay where they are. Town hall upgrades are now bought in build mode, and roads cost village resources.
- **Closing the game** (Exit or Alt+F4) now shows "Saving players..." for up to 3 seconds, so nobody loses progress.
- **Linux is experimental.** It needs a graphics card with Vulkan support. After unpacking, run `chmod +x Ravenfall.x86_64` (and `chmod +x RavenBot` for the Linux bot).

## What is new?

### A new connection to the server
The game now talks to the server over one connection instead of three.
- Every save is kept until the server confirms it. Saves that are not confirmed yet are written to disk and sent at the next start.
- A chat command is never run twice, even when it is resent after a reconnect.
- A dropped connection reconnects and catches up on what it missed. A missed inventory change repairs itself.
- If the new connection gives you trouble, start the game with `-network-v1` to use the old one.

### Sign in with Twitch
The login screen now starts with "Sign in with Twitch". The game shows a code, opens the website, and signs you in when you approve it there. A game password is no longer needed, and "Remember me" never stores your password.

### A new interface
Almost the whole interface has been rebuilt: player details, the player list, dungeon and raid panels, damage meters, combat numbers, island details, the game menu, the login and update screens.
- Windows can be dragged, resized and collapsed, and they remember where you put them.
- Change the size of the whole interface with Ctrl+= and Ctrl+-.
- Connection problems show as small warnings in the bottom left corner. Hover one to see what it means.
- After signing in, a "getting ready" card shows what the game is still waiting for.

### Languages
Every text on screen and every chat reply from the game can now be translated. **Swedish** is the first language. Pick it under Settings > Interface; the bot's replies follow it. Language files go in `StreamingAssets/Languages`, so new languages don't need a new build.

### Settings
Settings is a new window with tabs for Game, Graphics, Sound, Interface, Bot messages, Keys and Admin.
- New settings include window mode, resolution, vertical sync, frame rate limit, master volume, camera speed, and rebinding every shortcut.
- Every setting has a hint and a reset button.
- Toggle keys (hide logos, player details and so on) now remember their state between sessions.

### Visual overhaul
The islands look very different. Every new effect has its own graphics setting, and potato mode turns them all off.
- An ocean with waves that reaches the horizon, and boats that ride them.
- Volumetric clouds with shadows, haze, and stars at night.
- Dense grass with flowers, a richer terrain, and trees and plants that sway in the wind.
- Butterflies, gulls, fireflies, snow on Iceheim and cherry petals on Kyojin.
- Fires that light up their surroundings, turning windmills and smoking chimneys.
- Village houses whose windows glow at night while their owner is playing.
- An optional "miniature world" tilt shift (off by default).

### Weather
The weather now changes by itself between clear, fair, cloudy and overcast. Rain, heavy rain and thunderstorms roll in from upwind, with lightning and thunder, and the ground gets wet. On Iceheim it snows instead.
- `!weather` tells chat the current weather and what is coming.
- Moderators can bring in other weather with `!weather <clear|fair|cloudy|overcast|rain|heavy rain|storm>`.
- "Lightning flashes" can be turned off for viewers who are sensitive to flashing light.

### Moonlit nights
Nights are no longer pitch black. The moon lights the islands, torches reach further, and dungeons are lit by flickering torches. A new **Night brightness** setting lets you choose how dark nights get.

### Music and sound effects
- **Music now follows what is on screen**, with its own tracks for the menu, day, night, rain, sailing, each island, the tavern, the onsen, village building, the arena, dungeons, dungeon bosses, raid bosses and wars.
- **The world has sound.** You hear axes, pickaxes, splashing, cooking and crafting near the camera, plus wind, birds, crickets, waves, crackling fires, animals, and recorded rain and thunder.
- New setting: **Sound effects volume**.

### Village build mode
Press **BUILD MODE** on the town bar (Esc to leave).
- Place buildings anywhere in the village area, then move and rotate them.
- Move and turn the town hall.
- Town hall upgrades are now bought with village resources at village level 10, 30, 80 and 150.
- The area you can build in grows with each town hall tier, up to the whole island.
- Build mode shows which buildings are in the way.

### New town halls and houses
The home island has been reshaped into one big buildable plateau. All five town hall tiers are new, from a longhouse to a castle. Every building type has its own house that shows what it does, and each one hides a little surprise.

### Roads
Draw roads in build mode: dirt, gravel (town hall tier 2) or cobbles (tier 3). Roads curve through the points you click and blend into the ground. Each point can be wider or narrower, and the ends can be round or square. Roads cost village resources per metre, and you see the price before you build. Removing a road gives back half.

## Changelog

### Commands
- Added: `!weather`, see above.
- Added: `!market buy|sell|value` and `!help` in the local bot, plus the `!island`, `!skills`, `!stuck` and `!requirement` aliases.
- Updated: Join codes for raids and dungeons are now a setting under Game. When it is on, a wrong or missing word stops the join (before, the player joined anyway). Codes ignore case and spaces.
- Updated: Long chat replies and big loot announcements are split into several messages instead of being dropped.
- Updated: The exp multiplier end warnings share one message instead of three.
- Fixed: `!auto raid join` turned on dungeon auto-join instead of raid.
- Fixed: `!use exp scroll 5` used no scrolls.
- Fixed: `!upload logs` uploaded the game state instead of the log.

### Gameplay and balance
- Updated: Villages earn coins when their players defeat a raid or a dungeon (on top of player rewards). The coins can't be spent yet.
- Fixed: Village experience no longer freezes. Villages that were stuck level up as far as their stored experience reaches.
- Updated: Trees and gather spots get their level from where they stand. Away's trees are now level 50.
- Updated: Clan enchanting that gains several levels at once charges each level's real cost.
- Added: Buy from the vendor on the website (/shop).
- Updated: The vendor sells for at least 3 times what it pays, and never under 10 coins. Soulbound items can be sold to the vendor but don't come back into its stock.
- Updated: Marketplace listings expire after 21 days. A bound copy of an item can no longer be listed.
- Added: Clan bank, with daily allowances per rank (Member 50, Officer and above 500, owners unlimited).
- Fixed: Items with different names (for example enchanted ones) no longer merge into one stack, and tagged items such as streamer tokens keep their tag.

### Performance and stability
- Each player's character takes about half the memory and spawns three times faster.
- Trees and gather spots are drawn far more efficiently, with about 2,900 fewer objects in the world.
- Much less work per player per frame. The building picker no longer renders 14 cameras all the time (with 100 players this was two thirds of each frame).
- Server calls are much faster, and bot commands are handled up to 50 per frame instead of one, so busy chats no longer queue up.
- Server saves stay fast in long sessions, lose nothing that changes during a save, and one bad row no longer blocks the rest. Load tested with 1200 players for 30 minutes without a lost save.
- A game reconnecting no longer stops its stream's raids, dungeons and drops.

### Bug fixes
- Fixed: The update screen showed "VERSION" without the version number.
- Fixed: A burst of full volume when the game closes.
- Fixed: Players sent Home landed under the island, and players could return from a dungeon onto the sea, underground or into a tree.
- Fixed: Players teleported to the seabed at Eldara, and stuck players were pulled back to spawn every 5 seconds.
- Fixed: New players stood idle off the path for a minute after joining.
- Fixed: A ferry with mixed sailing levels broke `!train` and other task changes.
- Fixed: Fishing auto-train was stopped by the farming level.
- Fixed: Changing equipment could destroy weapons and capes.
- Fixed: "Collection was modified" errors in inventory repair, leaving raids and drop events.
- Fixed: Updating the game could sign the streamer out of "Remember me".
- Fixed: Trees, gather spots and the town hall could show up neon green, black or rainbow coloured.
- Fixed: Scroll zoom depended on the frame rate, and scrolling over a window zoomed the camera.
- Fixed: Clicking an enemy went through windows on top of it.
- Fixed: Island details showed the Health count for Healing.
- Fixed: The town bar counted the whole stockpile as gained per hour.
- Fixed: A game folder with "overlay" in its path started the game as an overlay.
- Fixed: Marketplace listing and buying, stash withdrawals, sending between your own characters, gifting and redeeming could duplicate or lose items or coins.
- Fixed: Crafting could take ingredients without crafting, or give back the wrong amounts.
- Fixed: After a Twitch rename the bot joined your old channel. Renames are now picked up automatically.
- Fixed: Streamers were sometimes told their own stream was not live, and the /bot page always said the bot was not in your channel.

### Website
- A new look across the website, with a phone-friendly menu.
- New pages: News, the vendor shop, the clan bank, an experience calculator, Patreon tiers, How to play and About.
- Your town page shows your plots, lets you choose who lives where, and can set every plot to one skill.
- Search on the command and item lists.
- Notifications when your marketplace item is bought.

### Bot
- The local bot has every command the hosted bot has, shows its version (0.9.5.0) when it starts, and now comes self-contained: no .NET install needed.
- Both bots: a more reliable link to the game; commands typed while the game reconnects are no longer lost (hosted bot).
