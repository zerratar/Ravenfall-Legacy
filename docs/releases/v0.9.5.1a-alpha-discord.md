# 🐦‍⬛ Ravenfall v0.9.5.1 is out!

🍺 **A cozy new tavern.** Rebuilt from the inside, properly lit, with a barkeep and guests who talk, and a quest giver who has nothing for you... yet. Try `!quest`.
🛒 **Tavern shelves.** Streamers can put their own items on shelves in the tavern and chat buys them with `!redeem <shelf>` (add an amount to buy more). The coins go to the **village treasury**, not the streamer, and buyers earn loyalty points. See what's for sale with `!shop`.
🐾 **The pet race actually races.** `!race` with two or more pets, and the leaderboards now hang on the tavern wall.
🛡️ **Your own clan logo and cape.** Clan owners on Patreon can upload a logo and a cape on the website (Clan > Look), with a live 3D preview, and pick the cape shape: long, pointed, short or tattered.
🔧 **Fixes:** dungeons no longer finish themselves during the countdown, brighter nights and softer firelight, no more floating town hall piles, and no more false "Connecting to the bot" warning.
🌐 **On the website:** stream cards and the towns list now show which game version each stream runs, so you can find one with the newest features.

**Streamers, please note:**
• The game updates itself when you start it.
• Running the bot yourself? Grab the new `RavenBot.7z` for `!shop` and `!quest`. If you use our hosted bot, you don't need to do anything.

📜 Full changelog and downloads: https://github.com/zerratar/Ravenfall-Legacy/releases/tag/v0.9.5.1a-alpha

Thank you for all the feedback on 0.9.5, keep it coming! 💜
