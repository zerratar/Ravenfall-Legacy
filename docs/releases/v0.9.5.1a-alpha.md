# Ravenfall v0.9.5.1a Release

**A cozy new tavern with shelves where streamers sell their own items to chat, a pet race that actually races, your own clan logo and cape, and a round of fixes from the first days of 0.9.5.**

> **Running the bot yourself?** Download the new `RavenBot.7z` (or `RavenBot-linux.7z`) for `!shop` and `!quest`. The bot most streamers use (the one hosted by us) is already updated.

---

## What is new?

### A new tavern
The tavern has been rebuilt from the inside: a bar, a hearth, a dining corner, a games corner and a room for the seasonal redeemables, with a layout that every theme shares. It is lit properly now too, in every theme.
- **People live there.** The barkeep, the guests and a quest giver talk in speech bubbles.
- **`!quest`**: the quest giver has nothing for you... yet. Quests are coming in a later update.
- The labels in the redeemables room are readable, without the black boxes behind them.

### Tavern shelves: the streamer's shop
Streamers can now sell their own items to chat. **The coins go to the village treasury, not to the streamer**, so every sale helps the whole stream.
- **Streamers:** click an empty shelf in the tavern. Pick an item from your stash or one of your characters in the stream, set a price per item, how many, for how long (1 hour to 30 days) and who may buy (everyone, your clan, or certain clan ranks), with an optional limit per viewer. Unsold items come back when the time is up, or when you take them down.
- **Viewers:** buy with `!redeem <shelf number>` or `!redeem <item name>`, and add an amount to buy more than one: `!redeem 2 50`. Buyers earn loyalty points with the streamer, 1 per 1,000 coins spent.
- **`!shop`** lists what is on the shelves. The streamer can also use `!shop remove <shelf>`, `!shop price <shelf> <coins>` and `!shop report`.
- The window has a Sales tab with everything sold and what it made for the village.
- 4 shelves with the first town hall, one more for every town hall upgrade, 8 with the castle.

### The pet race works
`!race` in the tavern now runs a real race: once two pets have joined, a countdown starts, the camera moves to the track, and the pets run with bursts of speed and the odd stumble. The tic-tac-toe and pet race leaderboards now hang on the wall.

### Your own clan logo and cape
Clan owners who support Ravenfall on Patreon can now upload their own clan logo and, separately, their own cape on the website, under **Clan > Look**. Until now both were the owner's Twitch picture.
- An editor to crop, zoom, rotate, flip and place the image, with a live **3D preview of the cape** as you edit.
- Pick the **cape shape** your members wear: long, pointed, short or tattered. Each clan rank can wear its own.
- Without a cape image, capes show your logo on dark cloth.
- Changes show up in running games within a minute. Custom logos also show (within the hour) for streamers who have not updated yet; cape images and shapes need this version.

## Changelog

### Commands
- Added: `!shop`, `!redeem <shelf> [amount]` and `!redeem <item name> [amount]` for the tavern shelves, see above.
- Added: `!quest` (and `!quests`).
- Updated: `!race` works, see above.

### Gameplay
- Fixed: `!dungeon start` could finish the dungeon on its own during the countdown, before anyone saw it.
- Fixed: Resource piles floated in the air around town halls of tier 2 to 4.
- Fixed: The pet race models floated above the table.

### Graphics
- Updated: Nights are brighter, and weather dims the moonlight less.
- Updated: Fires, torches and lamps give a softer, more natural light over a larger area, instead of a strong red glow.
- Fixed: The tilt shift setting also made the game more colourful. It only blurs now; colour is part of the post processing setting.
- Fixed: The old flat clouds still floated in the sky above the new ones.
- Fixed: The tavern was too dark in some themes.

### Interface
- Fixed: "Connecting to the bot" could stay in the bottom left corner while the bot was working fine.
- Updated: The shelf window closes once an item is on its shelf, and the listing panel is easier to read.

### Website
- Added: Clan > Look, see above. Admins get a page to review clan images, with a switch to check every upload before it is used.
- Added: The stream cards on the front page and on /streams, and the towns list, show which game version each stream runs, so you can find a stream with the newest features.

### Bot
- The local bot (0.9.5.1) has `!shop` and `!quest`, and `!redeem` mentions the shelves.
