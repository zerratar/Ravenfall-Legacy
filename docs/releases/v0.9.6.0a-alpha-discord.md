@everyone **Ravenfall v0.9.6.0a is out!** The biggest update yet: there is finally something to do together beyond training.

📜 **Quests** - daily and weekly quests for everyone (`!quest`), stream quests the whole chat works on, story chains, achievements and titles (`!title`). Streamers can post their own bounties and stream goals.

🏰 **Ravebridge and hero mode** - a new island the streamer plays by hand: walk, fight, roll, talk to the islanders, do their quests and hold the line against endless waves. Chat joins with `!ravebridge` and fights beside you with `!hero` (heal, taunt, cheer, revive) and votes you boons between waves. Daily challenge and leaderboards on the website. It rises from the sea the first time you open it 👀

⛵ **Your own boat** - from a rowboat to a Galleon. Sail with `!sail`, fish at sea, take friends aboard, and earn hulls, sails and flags (clan colours too). Try every look in the new boat designer on the website.

🍌 **The banana island** - monkeys guarding a banana pile (`!banana`), pirate treasure (`!dig`), a ridiculous boat regatta (`!regatta`) and a pirate raid (`!pirates`). Yes, there is a Bananarang.

🌳 **Village decor** - move or remove the village island's trees and place fences, lights, stalls, banners and statues. Viewers can gift decor with loyalty points (`!decor gift`).

Also: Ironhill reimagined with dwarves and a sleeping dragon, a new 3D character customizer and quest page on the website, `!gift` for multiple sets, and lots of fixes.

⚠️ Auto join for raids and dungeons costs coins again (it was free by mistake since 0.9.5).
🤖 Running the bot yourself? Grab the new RavenBot from the release. The hosted bot is already updated.
