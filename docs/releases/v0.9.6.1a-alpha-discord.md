@everyone **Ravenfall v0.9.6.1a is out!** Fixes from the first days of 0.9.6, plus a few treats.

🏆 **Be first!** The first stream in the world on the new version earns the **Trailblazer** title, and streams that update within ten minutes become **Early Birds** (`!title`).

⬇️ **Updates while you stream** - new versions download in the background and **Update now** restarts straight into the patcher. Turn on **Install updates automatically** and the game updates itself between raids and dungeons, with a minute's warning to chat. (Settings > Game > Updates, works from this version on.)

📜 **Quest tracker** - the stream quest, the village story and the streamer's goals and bounties always on screen. F7 or the quest window shows or hides it.

🐾 **Pets with a will of their own** - they follow you, run to catch up and sniff about instead of sticking to your side.

🥭 **Mangoes** on the banana island (Gathering 15): Mango Smoothie, Mango Salsa, Mango Tart, and the legendary **Suuuuper Juicy Mango**.

🏰 **Ravebridge** - skeletons in the swamp, quest circles on the maps, a Retreat button between waves, and viewers who actually fight and heal beside the hero.

🔧 Lots of chat command fixes (`!vendor sell`, `!unstuck`, `!arena`, `!equipment ring`, `!raid join`, `!leave ravebridge` and more), boats that go back to fishing after a raid, and a vendor page that no longer disconnects.

🤖 Running the bot yourself? Grab the new RavenBot from the release. The hosted bot is already updated.
