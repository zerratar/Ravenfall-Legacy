# Ravenfall v0.9.6.1a Release

**Fixes from the first days of 0.9.6, a quest tracker for the stream, pets with a mind of their own, mangoes on the banana island, and updates that download while you stream.**

> **Running the bot yourself?** Download the new `RavenBot.7z` (or `RavenBot-linux.7z`) for the command fixes below (`!leave ravebridge`, `!raid join`, `!equipment ring` and more). The bot most streamers use (the one hosted by us) is already updated.

> **Be first!** The first stream in the world on 0.9.6.1a earns the **Trailblazer** title, and streams that update within ten minutes of the release become **Early Birds**. Wear them with `!title`.

---

## What is new?

### Updates while you stream
- **New versions download in the background** while the stream goes on. A card in the bottom left corner shows the progress, then **Update now** restarts straight into the patcher.
- **Install updates automatically** (off by default): a finished download waits until no raid, dungeon, Ravebridge run, wave run, regatta or pirate raid is under way, tells chat the game restarts in a minute, and then updates. Not now skips it for that version.
- Both settings are in **Settings > Game > Updates**. They work from this version on, so 0.9.6.1a itself still installs the usual way.

### Update honours
The first stream on each new version and the Early Birds get a shout in chat, a golden update card, and titles with higher tiers for the regulars. The website's download page lists them.

### Quest tracker
The stream quest, the village story step (with chat's votes) and the streamer's goals and bounties are always on screen. Move it, fold it, and pick its background, text size and what it shows in the settings. **F7** shows or hides it, and so does the button in the quest window's header.

### Story choices as cards
The runestone's blessings are three readable cards: the bonus in big letters (+5% combat, crafting or gathering skills exp), what it helps and a Choose button. While chat votes, each card shows its votes, the leader and the time left.

### Pets with a will of their own
Pets no longer stick to your side. They walk after you with a little slack, run to catch up, look at you when you stop and sniff about while you stand still. On ferries, boats and in the onsen they stay close as before.

### Mangoes
Mangoes grow on the banana island (Gathering 15, only on the Mangrove island). Cook them into a **Mango Smoothie** (Cooking 40), **Mango Salsa** (Cooking 110) or a **Mango Tart** (Cooking 200, it can burn). And once in a long while, the legendary **Suuuuper Juicy Mango** drops.

### Ravebridge
- **Skeletons in the swamp**, the level 10 group Father Aereck talks about.
- **Quest circles:** soft red circles on the big map and the minimap where the hero's quests can be done.
- **Retreat:** a button between waves ends a wave run keeping the chest and the record, without leaving Ravebridge.
- **Viewers fight beside the hero.** Viewers training combat on the island attack the hero's target (or the wave enemy nearest the hero) instead of walking off to the goblins. Healers keep the hero alive first, and a viewer who falls in a wave run comes back beside the hero. Helpers are capped so the waves stay a challenge.
- Items the server gives the hero rise from the hero as pickups do (+1 Tin Ore).
- The hero no longer gets stuck on simple slopes or between the swamp pools.

## Improved
- Clicking a story's place (the monkey chief, the forge-master, the runestone, the banana island) opens its story and does the talk step at once.
- Quest delivery steps name their command: Bring 150 Logs with `!quest deliver`.
- A plain `!quest` while on a quest chain answers with the chain's step alone. `!quest list` shows everything.
- Wood Planks can be crafted (2 Logs, Crafting 1), for the village story's bridge.
- Dungeons and raids count once for a stream's shared goals and credit everyone in them.
- Windows move when grabbed by their top edge, and the old UI no longer steals clicks meant for windows over it.
- Story lines are bold and upright, easier to read on stream.
- **Website:** the item list, the marketplace and the vendor page show 50 rows at a time with a Show more button (the vendor page kept disconnecting for some players). The characters page says which stream a character is in and what it does there. New styles reach you after an update without Ctrl+Shift+R.

## Fixed
- **Chat commands:**
  - `!vendor sell` and `!vendor value` work.
  - Moderators can `!unstuck` a player.
  - `!highest` alone shows total level.
  - `!clan join` takes the clan's full name and says when it is not found.
  - `!gift` with coins works in either order.
  - `!arena leave`, `start`, `end`, `kick` and `add` work.
  - `!enchant req` never enchants.
  - `!equipment shield`, `amulet`, `ring` and `pet` show the equipped piece.
  - `!raid join` and `!dungeon join` alone join.
  - `!leave ravebridge` (or onsen, arena, ferry) leaves only that place, and an unknown word explains instead of leaving the game.
  - `!mine`, `!chop`, `!fish`, `!farm` and `!gather` with an item above your level keep your current task.
  - `!auto rest status` says inactive after `!auto rest off`.
  - Commands ignore the invisible characters some chat clients add to a repeated message (`!quest begin shipwright` said there was no such chain).
- Fishing from your own boat resumes after a raid or dungeon instead of sailing off.
- A rock or other gathering spot the path cannot quite reach is worked from the path's end instead of standing there doing nothing.
- Characters no longer curl into a T-pose for a moment when they leave combat.
- The Ravebridge window no longer shows the bot's routing tags as text.
- The Halloween harvest quest says any farming counts (there is no pumpkin crop).
- The village story's `!quest give` names the item and progress of the step it was brought for.
