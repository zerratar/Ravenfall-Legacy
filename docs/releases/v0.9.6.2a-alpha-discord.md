@everyone **Ravenfall v0.9.6.2a is out!** A small fix release.

⚔️ **Enemies at their real levels again** - since 0.9.6 most island enemies fought like level 3 bunnies. Gladiators, elven soldiers, vikings, samurai, dragons and pirates are back to full strength.

🪙 **Coins** - gifting all your coins no longer leaves you with minus the amount, and coins always show what the server has.

🎒 **Items** - no more items showing twice or going missing for a few seconds after buying, selling, crafting, enchanting or gifting. Gear from the marketplace and raids goes on once it arrives.

🏘️ **Village** - a town hall upgrade no longer shows the village's resources below zero.

🏆 **Be first!** The first stream on the new version earns the **Trailblazer** title, and streams that update within ten minutes become **Early Birds** (`!title`).
