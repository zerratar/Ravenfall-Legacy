# Ravenfall v0.9.6.2a Release

**A small fix release: the island enemies are back at their real levels, and coins, items and the village's resources always show what the server says.**

> **Running the bot yourself?** Nothing changed for the bot in this release; your current RavenBot keeps working.

> **Be first!** The first stream in the world on 0.9.6.2a earns the **Trailblazer** title, and streams that update within ten minutes of the release become **Early Birds**. Wear them with `!title`.

---

## Fixed

### Enemies at their real levels again
Since 0.9.6 most island enemies had fallen back to level 3: the gladiators on Atria, the elven soldiers on Eldara, the vikings, samurai, ninjas, dragons and pirates all fought like bunnies. They have their proper levels again (a gladiator is combat level 461, an elven soldier 654), so fighting on the higher islands is a real fight once more. Train on the island that fits your level.

### Coins
- Gifting all your coins could leave you with **minus** the amount you gave instead of 0. Fixed.
- The same kind of slip could show coins a little off for a while after buying or selling on the marketplace, quest and story rewards, the banana island rewards, joining raids and dungeons automatically, and auto rest. The game now always shows the coins the server has.
- Spending coins on regatta meddling or hero elites puts them on hold while the server answers, so the same coins cannot be spent twice.

### Items
After buying, selling, vendoring, gifting, crafting, cooking, brewing, enchanting, using an item or a scroll, an item or two could show twice or go missing for a few seconds. The game now waits for the server's own update instead of guessing, and gear bought on the marketplace or won in raids and dungeons goes on once it arrives.

### Village
Upgrading the town hall could show the village's wood, ore, fish and wheat below zero for up to half a minute, which blocked roads and decorations. The village now shows the server's numbers right after the upgrade.

### Clans
`!clan join` on a clan that takes members by request says the request was sent (its officers answer on the website), and on an invite only clan it says so.
