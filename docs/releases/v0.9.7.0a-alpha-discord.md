@everyone **Ravenfall v0.9.7.0a is out!** The new combat, a tower the whole stream climbs together, battle pets, and gear that dresses you for what you do.

🏆 **Be first!** The first stream in the world on the new version earns the **Trailblazer** title, and streams that update within ten minutes become **Early Birds** (`!title`).

⚔️ **The new combat** - your character keeps everything. Armor takes damage off instead of making blows miss, enemies go for whoever worries them most (tanks matter!), and every enemy shrugs off some of fire, ice, lightning and poison and is weak to others. Health is now called **Vitality**.

🌳 **Skill trees** - spend points with `!learn`, set your loadout with `!loadout`, and your character uses its skills on its own. Each tree ends in three capstones and you pick one. Gatherers, crafters and tamers get trees of their own too (**Gathering**, **Artisan**, **Beastmaster**). Plan builds at https://www.ravenfall.stream/skill-trees

🗼 **The tower** - `!tower` takes you in. Three floors, each a world with its own town and ten parts: objectives count while you play, mini bosses guard the parts and a floor boss opens the next floor. The fights follow your stream's size, so a small stream is not a slow one. Knocked out players get back up after 40 seconds and rejoin the fight. Train on cleared parts with `!tower part 3`, see the floor with `!tower map`. And the tower now stands in the sea south of Home: `!sail tower` to visit, where your stream's best climbers are carved in stone and the very best stands as a statue. Press **T** to switch the camera between the tower and the world.

🛡️ **Class gear and outfits** - thirteen lines of armor for your role, from Mage robes to Paladin plate and Beastmaster furs. `!equip tank` (or `mage`, `healer`, `rogue`...) dresses you for a role, and your outfit now follows what you train: robes for Magic, plate for Defense, whatever you like for Fishing. See `!outfit`.

💎 **Gems and sockets** - `!socket` a gem into your gear for stats or an element, `!gems combine` three into a better one.

🏰 **Dark Fortress gear and Legendary weapons** - Dreadforged weapons to craft, fortress armor for the top tiers, four fortress bosses in the tower, and five Legendaries for the strongest players: each with a power of its own (one is a hand cannon, and yes, it goes boom). Hall of Legends: https://www.ravenfall.stream/legends

🐾 **Battle pets** - `!pets starter fox`, `bear` or `fawn`, `!tame` creatures you fight, hatch eggs with `!pets hatch`. Twenty-six kinds hatch from eggs, from a honking goose to a snacking panda.

🌐 **On the website** - your character page's Equipment tab shows your character in 3D in its gear with its outfits, and the Inventory tab is rebuilt: grouped by kind, big or small tiles, or everything at once.

🪙 **Auto join** - patrons get free auto joins every day and a discount on the rest, anyone can cap the daily spend with `!auto limit 50k`, and auto join leaves players in the tower or on Ravebridge alone (climbers join a raid by hand with `!raid join` if they want to go).

🥚 Also: twelve new quest chains, messages in bottles and treasure maps (`!use bottle`), Ravebridge only opens when the streamer opens it, and some silly surprises. Do not trust April 1st.

📉 Enchanted gear shows lower numbers than before: an old bug counted some enchantments twice. Nothing about the items changed.
