@everyone **Ravenfall v0.9.7.0a is out!** The new combat, a tower the whole stream climbs together, and battle pets.

🏆 **Be first!** The first stream in the world on the new version earns the **Trailblazer** title, and streams that update within ten minutes become **Early Birds** (`!title`).

⚔️ **The new combat** - your character keeps everything. Armor now takes damage off instead of making blows miss, enemies go for whoever worries them most (tanks matter!), and enemies resist some of fire, ice, lightning and poison and are weak to others. Health is now called **Vitality** and still grows from all your combat training.

🌳 **Skill trees** - spend your skill points with `!learn`, set your loadout with `!loadout`, and your character uses the skills on its own. Gatherers and crafters get points and two trees of their own too (**Gathering** and **Artisan**: double finds, thrifty cooking, cheaper gem combining and more). Plan builds at https://www.ravenfall.stream/skill-trees

💎 **Gems and sockets** - `!socket` a gem into your gear for stats or an element, `!gems combine` three into a better one.

🗼 **The tower** - `!tower` takes you in. Clear each floor's quests, beat its boss, rest in the towns between. 30 floors to start, up through ice and above the clouds, and chat can vote on every choice when the streamer is away. With cutscenes!

🏰 **Dark Fortress gear** - Dreadforged weapons to craft, rare fortress weapons in the hardest chests, new helmets for six gear lines, fortress armor for the top Dark Knight, Barbarian and Mage tiers, and four fortress bosses in the tower with trophies of their own.

⚔️ **Legendary weapons** - five fortress weapons are Legendary, the best in the game, and they stay the best as new gear comes. Only the strongest can find one, from the hardest bosses. Each has a power of its own (one is a hand cannon, and yes, it goes boom), grows through five ranks as you fight bosses, and glows. See who found them first in the Hall of Legends: https://www.ravenfall.stream/legends

🐾 **Battle pets** - `!pets starter fox`, `bear` or `fawn`, `!tame` creatures you fight, hatch eggs with `!pets hatch`, and wake a pet you already own with `!pets wake raven`. Pets fight beside you, level up and evolve.

🥚 **Silly surprises** - Rob Boss finally picked up his brush, some islands have odd new residents, and gathering turns up odd things now and then (try `!use` on them). Do not trust April 1st.

🪙 **Auto join** - patrons get free auto joins every day and a discount on the rest. Anyone can cap what auto join spends a day with `!auto limit 50k`, and see what it cost on the website's dashboard.

📉 Enchanted gear shows lower numbers than before: an old bug counted some enchantments twice. Nothing about the items changed.

🤖 Running the bot yourself? Grab the new RavenBot from the release. The hosted bot is already updated.
