## Your character keeps everything

Levels, items, coins, all of it. What changes is how a fight plays out. The game shows a few pages about it the first
time you start it (and again from Settings > Interface > What's new), and the website has the full story:
[How combat works](https://www.ravenfall.stream/combat), [Skill trees](https://www.ravenfall.stream/skill-trees),
[The tower](https://www.ravenfall.stream/tower), [Battle pets](https://www.ravenfall.stream/pets).

## The new combat

### Health is now Vitality
The Health skill is called **Vitality** (`!train vitality`; `!train health` still works). Your health is 8 per Vitality
level plus what your armor pieces add, so the numbers look different from before. Nothing was taken away.

### Armor takes damage off
Armor no longer makes blows miss. It takes a share off every hit, so hits land, fights last, and heavy armor matters.

### Roles and threat
Enemies hit whoever worries them most. Defense and heavy armor hold their attention, Healing keeps the others standing,
everyone else deals the damage. A tank in front matters, most of all against bosses.

### Five elements
Physical, fire, ice, lightning and poison. Weapons and gems can carry element damage, enemies strike with elements too,
and each enemy shrugs off some and is weak to others. Fire burns, ice chills (five chills freeze), lightning jumps to the
next enemy and can stun, poison stacks. A word over a fighter shows when an effect starts (Burning, Frozen, Immune);
turn it off in Settings > Interface > Effect words.

### Skill trees
Your combat levels give you skill points. Spend them with `!learn` (or plan a build on the website), put active skills in
your loadout with `!loadout`, and your character uses them on its own in fights. `!tree` shows your points, `!respec`
gives them back (a Tome of Forgetting, or coins).

### Gems and sockets
Raw gems come from mining and gathering, more from the tower, raids and dungeons. Your clan enchants them, `!socket` puts
one into your gear for stats or an element, `!gems combine` turns three into a better one, `!unsocket` takes one out.

### Enchanted gear shows lower numbers
An old bug counted POWER, AIM and ARMOR enchantments twice: a Lionsbane Katana (173 power) with POWER 20% showed 241
power instead of 207. That is fixed, so enchanted gear shows lower numbers than in 0.9.6. Nothing about the item changed;
the old number was wrong.

## The tower

A climb of floors for the whole stream: `!tower` takes you in. Every floor has quests (defeat its monsters) and then a
boss (`!tower boss`). On a floor everyone fights at the floor's level at most, so a new character and a veteran face the
same fight. Floors 1 to 20 are open in two bands, the Overgrown Halls and the Frozen Vault, with more to come.

- **Towns between the floors** (Mossgate and Rimeholt): people to talk to, quests, a campfire to rest at, and a place to
  train every skill as on your island.
- **Chat decides when the streamer is away.** The streamer or a moderator decides at once; anyone in the tower can vote
  instead (`!tower boss`, `!tower climb`, `!tower close`). `!tower leave` takes only you out.
- **Cutscenes:** the tower opening its gate, each new band's town, each boss's first appearance with a taunt, and every
  boss falling in slow motion. Settings > Camera > Cutscenes turns them off.
- **Streamers who do not want it:** Settings > Game > World > Tower open to chat.

## Battle pets

Taming is a new skill. Pick a first pet with `!pets starter fox` (damage), `bear` (tank) or `fawn` (healer), tame
creatures you fight with `!tame bunny`, and hatch eggs from quests, raids, dungeons and the tower with `!pets hatch`. A pet
that is out fights beside you, levels up, learns skills and evolves. `!pets` shows yours. Clans have a new skill for them,
the Rookery.

## Also new
