## Your character keeps everything

Levels, items, coins, all of it. What changes is how a fight plays out. The game shows a few pages about it the first
time you start it (and again from Settings > Interface > What's new), and the website has the full story:
[How combat works](https://www.ravenfall.stream/combat), [Skill trees](https://www.ravenfall.stream/skill-trees),
[The tower](https://www.ravenfall.stream/tower), [Battle pets](https://www.ravenfall.stream/pets).

## The new combat

### Health is now Vitality
The Health skill is called **Vitality**. It still grows by itself from all your combat training (a third of your combat
experience), and `!train all` still trains Attack, Defense and Strength together. Your health is 8 per Vitality level plus
what your armor pieces add, so the numbers look different from before. Nothing was taken away.

### Armor takes damage off
Armor no longer makes blows miss. It takes a share off every hit, so hits land, fights last, and heavy armor matters.

### Roles and threat
Enemies hit whoever worries them most. Damage builds threat; the Threat attribute, the Bulwark tree, Taunt and fighting
in melee with a one-handed sword build more, so a tank can hold them while Healing keeps the others standing and
everyone else deals the damage. A tank in front matters, most of all against bosses. Raid bosses now say which element
they strike with, and clicking an enemy shows what it is weak to and what it resists.

### Five elements
Physical, fire, ice, lightning and poison. Weapons and gems can carry element damage, enemies strike with elements too,
and each enemy shrugs off some and is weak to others. Fire burns, ice chills (five chills freeze), lightning jumps to the
next enemy and can stun, poison stacks. A word over a fighter shows when an effect starts (Burning, Frozen, Immune);
turn it off in Settings > Interface > Effect words.

### Skill trees
Your combat levels give you skill points. Spend them with `!learn` (or plan a build on the website), put active skills in
your loadout with `!loadout`, and your character uses them on its own in fights. `!tree` shows your points, `!respec`
gives them back: the first time free, then for a Tome of Forgetting (every weekly quest and finished quest chain gives
one) or coins. `!tree cleave` tells you about a skill: when your character uses it and which weapon it needs.

Each tree ends in a **capstone** for a maxed skill (level 999): Unbreakable, Miracle Worker, Colossus, Perfect Form,
Deadeye, Archmage, Undying and Legend of the Hunt. One strong skill each, for 15 points.

### Gems and sockets
Raw gems come from mining and gathering, more from the tower, raids and dungeons. Your clan enchants them, `!socket` puts
one into your gear for stats or an element, `!gems combine` turns three into a better one, `!unsocket` takes one out. A gem
taken out, or socketed over, usually crumbles: you are told your chance to keep it first and add `confirm` to go ahead.

### Class gear
Armor for your path. Five new lines of gear give up some armor for what your role needs:

- **Mage** robes: magic power, cast speed and accuracy.
- **Healer** vestments: healing power, heals that also shield, shorter cooldowns and more health.
- **Ranger** leathers: ranged power, critical hits, attack speed and evasion.
- **Melee** mail: melee power, critical damage, attack speed and armor penetration.
- **Tank** plate: the armor of plate with more health, more threat and less damage taken.

Eight tiers each, from level 1 to 900 (Apprentice to Starcaller, Guard to Aegis, and so on), with two head pieces a
tier to choose your look from. They are made with Crafting from two new materials and a bar or more of metal: **Linen**,
woven from **Flax** (a new Farming crop), and **Leather**, tanned from **Hide** (found now and then while you train a
combat skill). `!req sorcerer robe` tells you what a piece takes.

`!equip all` knows your path: gear made for the skill you are training wins over plain armor of its level, and gear
made for another path is not put on by accident. Go fishing or chop wood for a while and what you wear stays on.

### Enchanted gear shows lower numbers
An old bug counted POWER, AIM and ARMOR enchantments twice: a Lionsbane Katana (173 power) with POWER 20% showed 241
power instead of 207. That is fixed, so enchanted gear shows lower numbers than in 0.9.6. Nothing about the item changed;
the old number was wrong.

## The tower

A climb of floors for the whole stream: `!tower` takes you in. Every floor has quests (defeat its monsters) and then a
boss (`!tower boss`). On a floor nobody fights above the floor's level, so a stream of veterans cannot flatten a boss. A
character far below a floor's level is told so on the way in. Floors 1 to 30 are open in three bands, the Overgrown Halls, the Frozen Vault and the Storm Spire above the clouds, with more to come.

- **Towns between the floors** (Mossgate, Rimeholt and Galewharf, a harbor on the clouds): people to talk to, quests, a campfire to rest at, and a place to
  train every skill as on your island.
- **Chat decides when the streamer is away.** The streamer or a moderator decides at once; anyone in the tower can vote
  instead (`!tower boss`, `!tower climb`, `!tower close`). `!tower leave` takes only you out.
- **Cutscenes:** the tower opening its gate, each new band's town, each boss's first appearance with a taunt, and every
  boss falling in slow motion. Settings > Camera > Cutscenes turns them off.
- **Tower votes need one voice.** When the streamer is away, one `!tower boss`, `!tower climb` or `!tower floor 7`
  from a player inside opens a vote that passes a minute later, or at once when most of the players who are at their
  keyboards vote too (idle players do not count). `!tower close` still takes most of them.
- **A tower panel on screen** while anyone is inside: the floor, its quests and their progress, the boss countdown, the
  boss's health in a fight, the town between floors, and what to type next.
- **Streamers who do not want it:** Settings > Game > World > Tower open to chat.

## Battle pets

Taming is a new skill. Pick a first pet with `!pets starter fox` (damage), `bear` (tank) or `fawn` (healer), tame
creatures you fight with `!tame bunny`, and hatch eggs from quests, raids, dungeons and the tower with `!pets hatch`. A pet
that is out fights beside you, levels up, learns skills and evolves. `!pets` shows yours. Clans have a new skill for them,
the Rookery. Taming stops when you tame a creature: one `!tame`, one pet, and `!tame` again for the next.

Every pet you already own can fight too: `!pets wake raven` (or just `!pets wake` for the one you wear). `!pet` still
picks your cosmetic pet, which shows whenever no battle pet is out.

## Also new

- **Twelve new quest chains** (`!quest chains`), from Captain Whiskers to The magpie's ransom, five of them long ones for
  high levels, and more daily and weekly quests: the tower, Taming, crafting, cooking, brewing, and one for a skill you
  have neglected.
- **Experience books:** a quest that would pay experience in a skill you have at level 999 gives a book instead,
  worth what the quest would have paid: a Small book for a daily, a Medium one for a quest chain, a Large one for a
  weekly. `!use gathering book fishing` reads it for a skill of its kind (Combat, Gathering, Artisan or Adventure).
- **The daily streak now pays.** Daily quests have always shown a bonus for days in a row (10% more coins and
  experience a day, up to 50% after five days), but it was never paid out. Now it is.
- **Gems on the website:** socket a gem or take one out from an item's window on your character page.
- `!cook`, `!craft` and `!brew` only suggest things that skill can make when a name is not found.
- **Auto join for patrons:** patrons get some raids and dungeons auto joined free every day and a discount on the
  rest, more with each tier (Mithril 6 raids and 4 dungeons free and 10% off, up to Phantom 25 and 18 and 50% off).
- **Your own auto join limit:** `!auto limit 50k` caps what auto join may spend a day for all your characters
  together (`!auto limit off` removes it, `!auto limit` shows what it spent). Also on the website's dashboard, which
  now shows what auto join cost you today and this week.
- **What's new** pages on first start, in the style of Ravebridge's introduction.
- **Hero mode:** the health orb showed the old maximum (1735 / 165) and healing and regeneration did nothing above it;
  fixed. The skill bar has a carved gold frame.
- The **Tome of Forgetting** has an icon.
