Decisions made by Claude on `!equip <role or line>` that the owner may want to change:
- A role's class gear goes on even when plate ranks higher (the first version left someone at Ranged 797 in Elder
  Atlarus plate after `!equip ranger`). `!equip all` still follows the ranking.
- tank, melee, ranger, mage and healer are roles, not lines: the best line for the skill wins, and the named line
  only among equals. So `!equip melee` gave Nightfall and `!equip healer` gave Archon here. Exactly the line is
  `!equip melee line`, `!equip healer line` and so on (any line's name with "line" after it).
- A line by name changes armor only and changes nothing when there is nothing of it to wear; a role with no class
  gear is a plain equip best.
- The outfit is remembered by the game only, until `!equip all`, the player leaving or the game restarting. While
  it is remembered a worn piece of it is only replaced by another piece of it. The outfits per skill of
  docs/outfits-design.md are where a lasting choice belongs.
- Short forms are understood too: atk, str, def, defence, range, heal.
- The server's equip best was changed for `!equip all` as well: it remembers what was worn before it takes
  everything off, and an enchanted item is worth its own stats plus its enchantment (it was the enchantment's part
  alone, which took a Phantom Ring with a Woodcutting enchantment off for a plain Archers Ring III).
- `!equip samurai` used to answer someone who owns the old Samurai Helmet with "did you mean Samurai Helmet?"; it
  is the Samurai line now, and the helmet takes its whole name.
- ROBot's `!unequip` with nothing after it told people to use `!equip`; it says `!unequip` now.
