# v0.9.7.0a playtest sheet

One session with real chat, in order. The riskiest things come first, so if the session ends halfway the half
that matters is done. Tick what worked; for anything that felt wrong, a few words on the line is enough ("boss too
easy", "panel covers the map"). The full list with every detail is section 1 of `v0.9.7.0a-checklist.md`; this
sheet is the same tests in the order to play them.

If a step would take too long to reach (a floor boss, floor 3), stop and tell Claude: the stream's tower progress
can be moved on the dev database between steps.

## What is left for the owner

The mechanical half was played with bots on 2026-11-26 (marked "bots, 2026-11-26" below; screenshots in
`docs/mockups/playtest-2026-11-26-bots/`). Chat went through a fake local bot connection inside the editor, so every
reply and announcement went through the game's real packet handlers and was read back, and nothing reached Twitch.
What only you can judge or do:

- **Feel**: the pace of a part, how hard the bosses are, the numbers below each question. No natural boss fight was
  lost in the session (8 of 8 won, 2 to 10 players).
- **Real chat through the bot from master** (section 0): not run (needs the Twitch login).
- **A floor boss's Finisher title**: the last blow went to a bot, which is no server character, so the title was not
  given. Needs a real player landing the last blow.
- **Quests** (section 6): not tested (needs a real quest completion on a streak day and a skill at 999).
- **Website signed in** (section 7): not tested.
- **The release build signed in**: a Windows build like the release one (CoreCLR, not Development, version left at
  0.9.6.2a) built in 16 minutes, 5.4 GB, with the 11 tower scenes in it; it started with `-localserver`, loaded the
  world and waited at the sign-in. It has no saved device token (only the editor has one), so it went no further.
  Sign in once yourself, then: a tower part, the sealed doors, two courtyard copies. The build reported three
  TextMesh Pro shader errors ("Couldn't open include file 'TMPro_Properties.cginc'" in TMP_SDF-Mobile-2-Pass and
  TMP_SDF SSD), and the started game logged "No sounds folder found" (data\sounds is not in a plain build).
- **Decisions** (no code changed for them):
  - Snowball, Nibbles' Big Sister has no attack animation at all: the home-bunny model has no attack trigger, so it
    swings every 2.5 s (measured 2.5 s between swings) while it plays its idle clip. Every other boss played its
    attack clip. Nibbles (1-1) and Zap (3-2) use the same model.
  - Boss attempts pay almost no combat experience: a level 802 bot made about 7,900 exp an hour in boss fights
    against 114,000 on the same part's monsters (numbers under section 1).
  - Normal monsters barely touch a group: see the threat numbers under the pace question.
  - A lone level 36 against The Wailing Matron (floor 2, part 4) stood 105 s without falling: the boss hit it for
    about 1 a swing and lost 1% of its health. A weak lone player can neither win nor lose until the time runs out.
  - A crowd on a cleared part cannot move to another cleared part while three are open, even its last player: their
    own part still counts as open (reply: the three open parts). They have to `!tower leave` or go to the climb
    first.
  - The player details window opens over the top of the players list when a player is picked (both top left), and
    the meters window under them (seen in Galewharf with all three up).
  - A bot training Attack in Galewharf shows "Not able to train here" in its details, yet it gained about 95,000
    exp an hour there (the town pays the island's rate).
  - The tower panel's player count counts everyone in the tower, the players list only the part being watched
    (30 against 5 in the session). Same kind of confusion as the town header.
