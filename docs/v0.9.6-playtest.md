# v0.9.6 playtest checklist

For one or two sessions against the local dev server. Features are ordered roughly by risk: the least played and
most tuned-by-guess first. **NEVER TESTED** marks things nobody has tried at all (not even with bots). "Bots only"
means it ran in play mode with bots or evals, never with real input or real chat.

Numbers under "Tuning" are first guesses from the docs. Note what feels wrong and we change them after.

---

## 0. Setup

### Local server
- [ ] Ports 80, 443 and 3920 are free.
- [ ] From RavenNest `src/RavenNest.Blazor`: `ASPNETCORE_ENVIRONMENT=Development dotnet run --no-launch-profile`. It
      runs against the dev DB copy on BEDROOM (the connection is in appsettings; do not paste appsettings anywhere).
- [ ] Startup log: `DatabaseSchema` lists every table and column it adds. Expect none or a few; errors here matter.
- [ ] Stop it with Ctrl+C in its console, not by killing it (a kill skips the last save).

### Editor
- [ ] Ravenfall > Use Local RavenNest Server is ticked (a build uses `-localserver` instead).
- [ ] Log in yourself.
- [ ] Admin debug buttons on the right edge (on by default in the editor; Settings > Admin > Dev Tools). They have
      Start/End Ravebridge (forced, works even if locked), Spawn a bot / 10 Bots, Control Bots/Players, and Chat
      Notifications ON/OFF.

### Chat
- [ ] The live remote bot does NOT have the v0.9.6 commands (`!hero`, `!regatta`, `!dig`, `!banana`, `!pirates`,
      `!boat look`, `!decor`, `!shop`, `!redeem`, `!quest ...`, `!title`). To test chat for real, run the local bot
      from RavenBot `master` (`dotnet run --project src\RavenBot`, settings.json with your bot account and channel).
      The game listens for it on port 4040. I have not checked how the local bot behaves against the local server.
- [ ] F11 drops the remote bot connection. While the remote bot is connected, things like finished island quests
      still post to your real chat; turn Chat Notifications OFF if you do not want that.
- [ ] Bots cannot type, cannot buy from shelves, and never get a hero chest. Ask Claude to join bots to Ravebridge,
      a regatta or a boat by eval, or to add a second real dev DB character to the session.

### Dev DB and local state worth knowing
- [ ] The Mangrove island is already unlocked for zerratar on the dev DB.
- [ ] Zerratar has a 5 wave hero best on the leaderboard and 1 hero chest item from earlier tests.
- [ ] Local state the game keeps (delete to start fresh): `mangrove-bananas.json` and `mangrove-regatta.json` in the

## 1. Hero mode (Ravebridge)

The biggest feature and the least played. You tried it three times on 2026-10-25/26; everything after that is
unplayed: the design review fixes, telegraphed moves, boss phases, boons, `!hero elite`, retreat, viewer scaling, the
new wave HUD and the daily challenge.

### Getting there fast
- [ ] Admin button Start Ravebridge > "Who plays?" > pick your own character (needed for the chest, titles,
      leaderboard and decor) or the Ravebridge character (gets 100 coins a wave instead of a chest).
- [ ] Or `!hero start` (broadcaster only, Ravebridge must be unlocked), `!hero end`.
- [ ] Or `!ravebridge start` (broadcaster or mod, unlocked only), then the Hero mode button on the Ravebridge drawer
      at the top.
- [ ] Or the goblin storm: server `api/admin/ravebridge-force?storm=true` (game event 56). It only makes you the hero
      if you touched keyboard or mouse in the last 3 minutes; otherwise it opens Ravebridge for chat only.
- [ ] Captain Hargrove in the castle courtyard: "Hold the line" (5 waves). After it is done he offers endless runs
      and today's challenge, and names the top three.

### Controls and feel (real mouse and keyboard)
- [ ] WASD moves relative to the camera; left click walks, holding it keeps walking toward the cursor.
- [ ] Left click on an enemy: from above it auto attacks until the enemy falls; over the shoulder every swing is yours.
- [ ] Shift + click swings while moving; held button keeps swinging; three swings make a combo (third hits 35% harder).
- [ ] Hit stop, small shake and blade trail on hits. **Not checked by hand:** trail look, hit stop at full frame rate.
- [ ] Space rolls (invulnerable 0.5 s, 3 s cooldown); a roll with no room flashes its slot.
- [ ] 1-4 skills, Tab / Shift+Tab switch style (1 s), V switches camera, Q potion, C details, I inventory, right mouse
      turns, scroll zooms.
- [ ] Esc closes an open window/talk/shop without leaving hero mode. In a run the first Esc warns, a second within
      3 s leaves (between waves it retreats and keeps chest and record; mid wave it abandons).
- [ ] Click a tree, rock, fish or farm patch: the hero gathers; a swing or walk ends it.
- [ ] Sight lines: roofs and walls between camera and hero turn shadow-only; stairs and bushes beside stay.
- [ ] Screen shake setting: Game > Observer > Screen shake (off in potato mode).

### Waves, enemies, bosses
- [ ] 8 s count, then waves from the two gates nearest you; the HUD says where from ("From the stone bridge and the
      swamp"). Gates: past both bridges' east ends, Al Kharid's toll gate, the swamp road, the mill road.
- [ ] Kinds by wave: goblins 1, warriors 3, ghosts 6, skeletons 7, skeleton knights 11. Boss every 5th wave in turn:
      Warchief Grubnash, the Swamp Golem, the Tormented One, Sir Rattlebones.
- [ ] Telegraphs: a red line or circle that fills from the enemy out; roll or step out to avoid. Readable at night?
- [ ] Boss at 50% calls help, at 25% enrages (ENRAGED pulse on the boss panel).
- [ ] Stuns on bosses last half as long and the boss shrugs stuns off for 6 s after one.
- [ ] Stuck enemies get moved along their path after 2 s.
- [ ] Late waves (past 15) should end in a real fight.

**Tuning:**
- Wave size 3 + 2 per wave + some per viewer, at most 12 alive. Breather 15 s with 25% health and 40 mana back.
- Enemy health = the hero's best style's average hit x 2.5 (goblin), 30 to 50 for a boss, growing per wave, plus 60%
  of the fighting viewers' hits (capped at 8x the hero's).
- Blows: goblin about 1% of hero health, boss 5-6%, +5% a wave on top past wave 15.
- Lunge (warriors, knights) 7 m, 0.7 s warning, 3.5%; bolt (ghosts) 18 m, 0.8 s, 2.5%; boss slam 4.5 m, 1.2 s, 18%.
- Enraged: 35% faster, slam every 4-5 s with 0.9 s warning, blows +30%.
- Mana 100 + style level / 5 (max 300), 3 a second, +4 per basic hit that lands.
- Potion 35% heal, 3 charges, 6 s between drinks, refilled each boss wave.
- Heals: Mend 20% to the hero, 12% to allies; Sanctuary 3% a tick; `!hero heal` 6% (healer) or 2% (bandage).
- Kit ranks at style level 400, 600, 800, 999: +15% damage and healing each ("Rank II" on the HUD).
- Skills unlock at style level 1, 30, 100, 250.

### Chat's part (local bot or a second account)
- [ ] `!hero` joins and fights beside the hero (same as `!ravebridge`). `!ravebridge leave`, `!rb` works as a short form.
- [ ] `!hero heal`, `!hero taunt`, `!hero cheer`: the feed under the wave bar and the top helpers panel update.
      No chat reply while the command is on cooldown (by design).
- [ ] `!hero revive` / `!rb revive` while downed: a pip per vote needed, red screen frame, BACK UP banner.
- [ ] A healer standing within 12 m revives too.
- [ ] Solo (no viewers on the island) the run ends 4 s after going down.
- [ ] `!hero 1`, `!hero 2`, `!hero 3` in the breather: the boon panel shows votes; the winner applies as the wave starts.
- [ ] `!hero elite`: first send tells the price, second within 30 s pays (1500 coins, needs the RavenNest spend
      endpoint). The elite comes from a gate, bigger and tougher.
- [ ] A quarter of ordinary enemies go for a viewer within 25 m; viewers feel the hits.
- [ ] Viewers fighting get combat exp each wave held.
- [ ] **NEVER TESTED:** the chat replies of `!hero` commands through a real bot.

**Tuning:**
- Revive: 20 s, 30% of viewers on the island (1 to 5); each revive needs +1 vote and gives 4 s less (min 8 s).
  Healer revive 5 s + 3 s per earlier revive.
- Cheer +5% skill damage for 20 s, stacks to 10, +5 mana, once per 30 s per viewer (does not boost the basic attack).
- Taunt: up to 3 enemies within 15 m of the hero, 6 s, viewer takes half damage, once per 30 s.
- Heal cooldown 10 s (healers: Healing 20 or training it), bandage 30 s.
- Viewer blow = 60% of the kind's share, taken from the viewer's own health.
- Boons: Sharpened steel +15% dmg, Deep wells +40% mana back, Thick hide -12% taken, Second wind (potions refilled
  +1), Glass cannon +30% dmg / +20% taken, Swift feet (roll 0.6 s sooner), Blood price (3% of damage back).
- Elite: 1500 coins, 4x tough, hits 60% harder, boss loot table, 2 a wave, 1 per viewer.
- Viewer exp per wave: factor 2 + half the wave.

### The wave HUD (new 2026-11-02)
- [ ] Before wave 1: the gathering plate, no "Wave 0 held!".
- [ ] Badge: gold, red on boss waves, green HELD in the breather; enemies left, fallen, progress bar, where from.
- [ ] Track: countdown, pips with skull on boss waves, "Boss in 2" / "Boss next" / best so far.
- [ ] Banners one at a time: HERE THEY COME, BOSS WAVE, WAVE HELD, BOSS DOWN, NEW BEST, BACK UP.
- [ ] Result card: held / retreated / broke, counting stats, NEW BEST, rank, titles, chest, 15 s timer bar.
      Abandoned runs show no card.
- [ ] With viewers joined: nothing overlaps (party and bounties in the left column, helpers under the minimap, boon
      box, boss panel, down panel). Try at a small and a large UI scale.

### Daily challenge (game 1592a49, RavenNest 0c5f0488)
- [ ] Captain Hargrove offers "Today's challenge: <twist> and <twist>", explains the twists, your best today and
      today's top three.
- [ ] Blue TODAY'S CHALLENGE strip under the track; boss pips follow Boss rush.
- [ ] Result card says "#N in today's challenge" and "Best today".
- [ ] Does not touch the all-time board, titles or decor; does give the chest and stream quest progress.
- [ ] A run that ends after midnight UTC counts for the day it started.
- [ ] **NEVER TESTED** by hand or in a playtest.

**Tuning:** Swarm (x1.5 enemies, a third weaker, 16 alive), Thick hides (a quarter fewer, 40% tougher), Boss rush
(boss every 3rd wave), Hasty (+25% speed), Elite guard (elite mid wave from wave 3), Hunters (half go for viewers),
Dry flasks (no potions on boss waves, half health back between waves), Tiny terrors (60% size goblins), Blessed (start
with the day's boon).

### Rewards, record, titles
- [ ] 5+ waves with your own character: chest on the result card; top 3 helpers get one each.
- [ ] Wave 10, 25, 50: titles "the Gatekeeper", "the Shield of Ravebridge", "the Unbreakable" announced in chat, plus
      decor (Hero's banner, Goblin war drum, Hero of Ravebridge statue). **NEVER TESTED live:** titles and the decor
      announcement.
- [ ] Rank shown on the result card; leaderboard via Captain Hargrove.
- [ ] Stream quest "Hold 10 waves on Ravebridge in hero mode" (only offered with Ravebridge unlocked). **NEVER TESTED.**

**Tuning:** chest tier by waves 5 common, 10 uncommon, 15 rare, 25 epic, 40 legendary; hero gets 1 + 1 per 10 waves
(max 5); one chest per session every 10 minutes. Server caps a run at one wave per 12 s.

### Loot, shop, bounties, hero gear
- [ ] Kills drop coins, pouches and bottles with labels; uncommon+ have a coloured beam; walk over to pick up.
- [ ] I opens the inventory: hero gear slots, Ravebridge bag, backpack. Right click wears/removes gear.
- [ ] Shopkeeper at the general store: "Show me your wares". Buy, sell, reroll gear (Sell tab).
- [ ] Bounties after each NPC's story quest: offer, reminder with counts, tracker in the hero HUD, hand-over, cooldown.
- [ ] Bag, gear and upgrades are still there after a restart.

**Tuning:** draught 25, whetstone 60 (+15% dmg 5 min), stew 60 (-15% taken 5 min), potion belt 300/750/1500, light
boots 500/1200, lucky charm 400/1000, mystery satchel 350, reroll 2x worth (+1x per earlier reroll). Gear drop 2.5%
(most), 6% (knights, souls), 1% (cows), bosses always. Bounty Old Bridgewell's tusk has a 30 min cooldown.

## 2. Regatta (banana island)

Balance changed on 2026-11-02 and is unplayed. Ran with 8 bots and a fake chat client only.

### Getting there fast
- [ ] A character with an own boat equipped (finish the shipwright chain with `!quest begin shipwright` for the
      Rowboat, or give a test character a boat on the dev DB).
- [ ] `!regatta` joins (opens a 90 s sign-up). `!regatta start` (streamer/mod) starts now without your character.
- [ ] Needs 2 racers; a forced start with 1 is a time trial.
- [ ] Ask Claude to add bot racers by eval for a full field.

### What to try
- [ ] Start: sails furled at the line, 3-2-1, war horn, all set sail at once.
- [ ] Boats sail in lanes beside each other, not through each other.
- [ ] Bottles: tailwind, cannonball, peel, shield; icons in the standings on the right.
- [ ] Reefs and wreckage; higher Sailing steers round more often.
- [ ] Two laps; after the winner the rest get 25 s, then are placed as they lie.
- [ ] Race camera (Game > Observer > Regatta camera): start line, leader, battles, buoy cams, the dock cam. Any camera
      key takes it back.
- [ ] Viewers: `!regatta keg [name]`, `!regatta wind [name]` (free once a minute without a name; paid ones reply with
      the price, send again within 30 s). `!regatta kraken` (streamer/mod, once a race).
- [ ] Paid meddles put half in the pot: "Chat's meddling put N coins in the prize pot", paid to the next podium.
- [ ] Prizes, Sailing exp and sails (looks) at the end; board at the jetty.
- [ ] `!regatta leave`, and `!sail` / `!disembark` / `!fish` mid race drops you out.
- [ ] **NEVER TESTED:** real chat meddling, paid meddling against the deployed spend endpoint, ships mooring at the
      finish (every ship lies at the one Mangrove dock point for 20 s, likely overlapping).

**Tuning:**
- Race speed `30 + (own speed - 12) * 0.25` m/s (rowboat 30, master's galleon 44.5). Course radius 150 m.
- Bottles by place: front third shield 40 / peel 45 / tailwind 15; middle tailwind 30 / cannon 30 / peel 20 /
  shield 20; back third tailwind 45 / cannon 40 / shield 15. Back tailwind 1.7x for 5 s, others 1.5x for 4 s; 15% of
  back cannonballs are chain shots at the leader.
- Hits: cannon 45% speed 2 s, keg spin + 35% 2.6 s, peel spin + 50% 1.4 s, reef 40% 1.6 s, wreckage 60% 1.2 s,
  Kraken 15% 3.2 s. 4 s immunity after a hit.
- Dodge chance 20% at Sailing 1 to 80% (about 50% at 100, 70% at 300).
- Meddling: free every 60 s, 500 coins random, 2000 aimed (doubling per aimed one: 4000, 8000), kegs per race
  2 + racers, wind at most every 8 s and twice per racer.
- Prize `(500 + Sailing * 5)` x place factor + pot share 50/30/20. New sail: winner always, 2nd/3rd 30%, others 10%.
- Sign-up 90 s, 10 min cooldown, 12 racers max.
